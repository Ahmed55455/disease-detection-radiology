🩻 X-ray Capture

Take a photo of a chest X-ray. It is uploaded, analyzed and added to the radiologist worklist.

⚕️ This tool assists radiologists and does not replace them. It is not a medical diagnosis. The model was trained on digital X-ray images, so results on phone photos can be less reliable.
Tips for a good photo
  • Lay the film flat or hold the phone parallel to the screen.
  • Fill the frame with the chest; avoid the room around it.
  • No glare or reflections; turn off the flash.
  • Hold still so the photo is sharp.
